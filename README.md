## Research

**M.Sc. in Computer Science (Visual Computing)** — Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) · CNPq Scholarship Holder · *Mar 2025 – present*

Comparative study of CNNs and Vision Transformers for open-set, pixel-wise, spatio-temporal classification of vegetation species in the Brazilian Cerrado biome, using multi-temporal UAV and near-surface tower imagery across Serra do Cipó and Itirapina.

**Publication**

A. R. Gomes et al., "Efficient Spatio-Temporal Vegetation Pixel Classification with Vision Transformers," *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (JSTARS)*.
